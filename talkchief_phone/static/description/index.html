TalkChief Phone

Make and answer TalkChief calls without leaving Odoo

TalkChief is a cloud phone system: business numbers, call queues, call recording and AI call summaries. This module adds a TalkChief item to Odoo's top bar. It opens the TalkChief calling panel, where your agents sign in to TalkChief and make and answer calls through their computer or their TalkChief phone.

What your agents get

  • Calls from Odoo's top bar. The TalkChief panel opens in the corner of any Odoo page and stays available while you work.
  • Click to call. Click a phone number on a lead, opportunity or contact and it goes into the TalkChief dial pad. Check it and click Call.
  • The caller on incoming calls. When a call comes in, the panel opens with the caller's name and their lead or contact. Your Odoo page stays as it is; click Open record when you want to see the record in Odoo.
  • Every call in the chatter. With the TalkChief integration connected, each call is logged as a note on the matching lead or contact: direction, result, agent, talk time, a link to the recording, the agent's note and outcome, and the AI call summary if you turn it on.
  • New leads for new numbers, if you turn it on.

What you need

  • A TalkChief account, with a phone line for each agent.
  • Your Odoo database connected in the TalkChief web app (Integrations, CRM, Odoo) with an Odoo API key. Call logging uses Odoo's external API.
  • Odoo 18, 19 or 20, with the CRM app.

Search for "Odoo" in TalkChief's help center for the step-by-step setup guide.

External service and your data

This module needs the TalkChief service, which runs outside Odoo. It shows TalkChief's calling panel (app.talkchief.io) inside Odoo.

  • Nothing happens until you opt in. The panel loads, and phone numbers you click go to it, only after a user opens TalkChief from the top bar. This browser remembers that choice.
  • What the module sends to TalkChief: only the phone numbers you click.
  • What TalkChief sends to Odoo's page: only which lead or contact to open, when you click Open record.
  • The module contains no Python code and stores nothing in Odoo. Agents sign in to TalkChief in the panel; Odoo keeps no TalkChief sign-in details.
  • The panel works only in the Odoo database your TalkChief company is connected to.
  • Odoo never places a call by itself: a clicked number only goes into the dial pad.
